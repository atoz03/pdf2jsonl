【Research Content 1】 Biological breeding scenarios, knowledge and data aggregation and governance.

Topic 1 focuses on breeding knowledge organization and data governance, unfolding across three levels: data standards, ontology construction, and knowledge fusion. In data standards and governance, it combs through multi-source breeding data types and establishes metadata specifications and a quality evaluation system. In ontology construction, it designs a domain ontology covering multi-level concepts and relationships such as gene-trait-environment-variety. In knowledge fusion, it develops methods for knowledge extraction, entity alignment, conflict resolution, and dynamic updates, constructing an AI-Ready breeding knowledge base to form a traceable, evolvable high-quality data foundation.

【Research Content 2】 Construction of specialized scientific agents embedded with breeding knowledge.

Topic 2 focuses on building the core capabilities of breeding scientific agents, researching an agent framework integrating reasoning, planning, memory, and autonomous execution. The reasoning layer compiles breeding knowledge, population genetic laws, and experimental feedback into structured research states, constructing a knowledge state machine to constrain scientific hypothesis generation and causal inference. The planning layer decomposes tasks and designs experiments based on research states and historical trajectories, generating executable plans. The memory layer designs a hierarchical memory architecture to manage long-range breeding knowledge, mid-range experimental states, and short-range operation contexts respectively. The execution layer interfaces with skill APIs to complete tool invocation, result verification, and strategy correction, forming a closed-loop continuous optimization.

【Research Content 3】 Skillification technology for breeding models and tools.

Topic 3 focuses on the skill-based encapsulation and integration of breeding tool models, defining standardized breeding component interaction interfaces and unifying interface rules for data input, parameter configuration, workflow orchestration, and visualization. It automatically generates adaptive interfaces based on the agent work plan, connecting the full chain of planning, operation, computation, and results. Standardized encapsulation of multi-dimensional breeding analysis tools covers models such as genomic prediction, genome-wide association analysis, and population genetic analysis, defining standard inputs, outputs, and parameter semantics. It builds a skill registration center supporting LLM-driven intelligent orchestration and the structured flow of scientific hypothesis cards, experimental design cards, and result analysis cards.
