Materials and Methods
Twelve-day-old seedlings were treated at 42 C for 14 h and survival was scored after 7 days of recovery. Each line was tested in 3 biological replicates of 24 plants. Survival rates were compared with a two-tailed t test. Split-luciferase assays were performed in Nicotiana benthamiana leaves.
